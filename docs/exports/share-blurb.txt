•  *Broken volunteer link* — it currently dead-ends.
•  *Dead analytics tag + "© 2020" footer* — small cleanups.
•  A couple of security hygiene items — not urgent, the host already blocks attacks.

Bigger picture: our top page by far is the Colorado BRT comment guide — people spend about a minute on it. That action-guide format is worth repeating on other campaigns.
