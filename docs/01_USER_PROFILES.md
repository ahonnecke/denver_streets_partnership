# User Profiles

Nine profiles. Each profile defines: who they are, what they came for
(job-to-be-done), what success looks like for them on this site, and the
primary CTA they should hit.

Profiles are not mutually exclusive — a single person may move between them
over time (e.g., Curious Newcomer → Engaged Advocate → Donor → Coalition
Member). Goal G2 in `02_GOALS.md` is about making that progression frictionless.

---

## P1 — Curious Newcomer

**Who:** Heard about DSP from a friend, social post, or news story. Knows
nothing about transportation policy. Maybe just got hit by a near-miss while
walking the kids to school.

**Job-to-be-done:** "Tell me what this organization is and whether I agree
with it, in under 60 seconds."

**Site success:** Lands on homepage, reads one sentence of mission, sees one
current campaign, clicks subscribe or learn-more.

**Primary CTA:** Newsletter signup (low-commitment).

**Secondary:** Read one campaign page. Follow on social.

---

## P2 — Engaged Advocate

**Who:** Already on the email list. Has signed at least one petition.
Probably attended one event. Per CSV: this segment receives 2-3× higher
engagement on follow-up emails than the full list.

**Job-to-be-done:** "What can I do this week? What's urgent?"

**Site success:** Goes directly to "Take Action," sees current asks ranked by
urgency, takes the action in under 2 minutes (mobile or desktop).

**Primary CTA:** Take an advocacy action (Muster), RSVP to event.

**Secondary:** Share with their network. Recruit a friend.

---

## P3 — Hyperlocal Resident

**Who:** Has a specific street/intersection problem. Frustrated. Not (yet) a
movement person. Searched something like "report dangerous crosswalk Denver."

**Job-to-be-done:** "Help me fix THIS intersection. Tell me who to call,
what to say, what's already been tried."

**Site success:** Finds Fix My Street Guide. Finds prior advocacy on their
corridor (e.g., Federal, Colfax, Colorado, Peoria). Joins email list with
their address; gets routed to neighborhood-relevant updates.

**Primary CTA:** Fix My Street Guide download/walkthrough; address-tagged
newsletter signup.

**Secondary:** See what their council district has on the docket.

---

## P4 — Donor / Recurring Funder

**Who:** Already aligned with the mission. Has disposable income. Wants
their money to matter. May be evaluating year-end giving against multiple
nonprofits.

**Job-to-be-done:** "Show me what you accomplished last year. Show me what
this year's $100 buys. Make giving frictionless and recurring."

**Site success:** Reaches Impact page, sees quantified wins by year. Reaches
donate page, sees suggested amounts mapped to outcomes ("$50 = 10 yard
signs"), completes recurring gift in under 90 seconds.

**Primary CTA:** Recurring donation via FundraiseUp.

**Secondary:** Tax-deductible receipt; share their gift; view donor wall
or impact updates.

---

## P5 — Volunteer / Action-Team Member

**Who:** Wants to give time, not (only) money. Available evenings or
weekends. May want a recurring volunteer role or a single-event slot.

**Job-to-be-done:** "Show me opportunities that fit my time and skill. Sign
me up. Tell me where to be and what to bring."

**Site success:** Volunteer page lists opportunities with time commitment,
location, and skill needed. One-click signup. Follow-up email confirms.

**Primary CTA:** Volunteer signup form (currently Mailchimp-hosted survey).

**Secondary:** Join an action team (Colorado Blvd, Federal, etc.).

---

## P6 — Coalition Member / Partner Org

**Who:** Staff at a partner nonprofit, neighborhood association, or business
group. May be a Denver Streets Congress member.

**Job-to-be-done:** "Stay aligned with DSP's positions. Find sign-on
letters. Co-promote events. Reference DSP's research in our own work."

**Site success:** Finds recent sign-on letters. Subscribes to a coalition
list (separate from public newsletter). Downloads logo, position statements,
shareable graphics.

**Primary CTA:** Apply to / log in to Denver Streets Congress.

**Secondary:** Co-branding kit. Sign-on letter library.

---

## P7 — Journalist / Media

**Who:** On deadline. Covering a transportation story. Needs a quote, a
contact, a stat, a recent statement.

**Job-to-be-done:** "Find the right person to call. Get a recent quote I
can use. Verify a fact about Denver streets."

**Site success:** Finds press kit (bios, headshots, contact info, recent
press releases) within 30 seconds of landing.

**Primary CTA:** Press contact form / direct email + phone for media
inquiries.

**Secondary:** Browse press releases. Pull recent quotes. Find executive
bio for attribution.

---

## P8 — Researcher / Student / Policy Wonk

**Who:** Grad student writing a thesis. Urban planning student. CU Denver
researcher. State or federal staffer doing literature review.

**Job-to-be-done:** "Find DSP's published research, studies, data, and
position papers. Cite them. Build on them."

**Site success:** Searchable, filterable resource library by topic, year,
type (study, position paper, sign-on letter, presentation). Direct PDF
download links. Citation metadata visible.

**Primary CTA:** Resource library search / filter.

**Secondary:** Subscribe to research-only updates.

---

## P9 — Decision-Maker / Government Staffer

**Who:** City Council aide, DOTI staffer, RTD staffer, mayoral office.
Wants to know DSP's stance and pressure level on a specific issue.

**Job-to-be-done:** "Where does DSP stand on [specific bill/project]?
How loud is their base on this? Who has signed on?"

**Site success:** Finds DSP's position statement on the issue. Sees number
of advocacy actions taken (signal of base intensity). Sees coalition
sign-on signatures.

**Primary CTA:** None directly; this profile reads, doesn't convert. Goal is
to make positions and base size visible.

**Secondary:** Read recent letters to officials.
